About Us

Dr. Md. Afzal Mahfuzullah

Vitreo-Retina Specialist & Phaco Surgeon | MBBS, MCPS, FCPS (Ophthalmology), Fellow — Vitreo-Retina | BMDC Reg: A-48558

Comprehensive Eye Care

Dr. Mohammad Afzal Mahfuzullah, MBBS, MCPS, FCPS (Ophthalmology), Fellow — Vitreo-Retina, is currently working as Associate Professor (Vitreo-Retina), Department of Ophthalmology, Bangladesh Medical University (BMU) and Consultant Ophthalmologist at Vision Eye Hospital, Green Road, Dhaka, Bangladesh. He currently holds the post of Joint Secretary General, Bangladesh Vitreo-Retina Society (BVRS). His working fields of interest are Cataract, Retina & Laser surgery in ophthalmology.

Dr. Afzal won the Best Abstract Award at the Asia Pacific Vitreo-Retina Conference held in Gold Coast, Australia on 27 August 2026. He won the prestigious M A Matin Gold Medal in 2021 for the best research work at the Ophthalmological Society of Bangladesh (OSB) Conference, and the Best Paper Award at the OSB Conference 2017. He has several presentations to his credit at national and international conferences (Bangladesh, USA, Spain & Australia).

He is also a Fellow in Retina and Vitreous (IEH), completed a long-term Fellowship in Vitreo-Retina (Bangladesh & India), advanced training in vitreoretinal surgery at Susrut Eye Hospital, India, and Advanced Vitreo Retinal Microsurgical Training in Gold Coast, Australia.

Dr. Afzal is a life member of the Ophthalmological Society of Bangladesh (OSB), Bangladesh Vitreo-Retina Society (BVRS), Bangladesh Uvea Society, Bangladesh Society of Cataract and Refractive Surgeons (BSCRS), Bangladesh Society of Low Vision (BSL), Bangladesh Medical Association (BMA), Old Laboratorian Association (OLSA) and Bangladesh Academy of Ophthalmology (BAO), and a member of the European Vitreo-Retina Society (EVRS).
