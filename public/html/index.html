About Dr. Md. Afzal Mahfuzullah

Vitreo-Retina Specialist & Phaco Surgeon

Dr. Mohammad Afzal Mahfuzullah — MBBS, MCPS, FCPS (Ophthalmology), Fellow — Vitreo-Retina — is currently working as Associate Professor (Vitreo-Retina), Department of Ophthalmology, Bangladesh Medical University (BMU) and Consultant Ophthalmologist at Vision Eye Hospital, Green Road, Dhaka. He currently holds the post of Joint Secretary General, Bangladesh Vitreo-Retina Society (BVRS). His working fields of interest are Cataract, Retina & Laser surgery.

He won the Best Abstract Award at the Asia Pacific Vitreo-Retina Conference, Gold Coast, Australia (27 August 2026), the prestigious M A Matin Gold Medal (2021) for best research work and the Best Paper Award (2017) at the Ophthalmological Society of Bangladesh (OSB) Conference. He has several presentations at national and international conferences (Bangladesh, USA, Spain & Australia). BMDC Reg: A-48558.
